Why a meter, not a microphone

The current icon loses its grille slots and chevron below 64 pt because the metaphor needs detail to survive. Three rounded bars have nothing to lose — the 16 pt Finder rendering above is the same drawing, not a simplified variant. It also stops looking like every other audio utility: this is not a mic, it is what the mic is doing.

Three rects, one rounded rect, two linear gradients — expressible directly in CoreGraphics, so make_icons.py keeps its job. The single blue peak is the only chromatic element and it is the same hue as the app's accent.

Constraints honoured

  • Every glyph is one colour on transparent at 20 × 20 pt — valid NSImage template images, so macOS still inverts them for dark menu bars and open menus.
  • The recording red dot is the single template opt-out, exactly as the app does today.
  • All six are distinguishable by shape, not colour — which is what matters when the icon is white on a bright wallpaper.
  • None of this is load-bearing: the menu bar can hide the item at any moment, so the overlay and sound cues carry the same six states independently.
