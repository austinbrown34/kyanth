shout

Local push-to-talk dictation for macOS. Hold a key, speak, release — the words appear in whatever you were typing into, and no audio leaves the Mac. shout is a meter: the mark is amplitude, not a microphone.

Everything is live

Hold ⌥ to dictate on the overlay and setup screens. Press Right ⌥ + Right ⌘ on Settings — Shortcut to watch the reception pill light. Type in the History search field, switch the outcome filters, click a row to expand it. Use Light / Dark above — every screen carries both appearances.

Input level and transcription latency are simulated stand-ins for AVAudioEngine and the local model. Everything else is the app's own behaviour.
