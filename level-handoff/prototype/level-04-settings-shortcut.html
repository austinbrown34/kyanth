Shortcut

What shout listens for, and proof that it is hearing it.

Activation

Recording runs for exactly as long as the keys are down. Release to transcribe.

Anything shorter returns the low blip instead of an empty transcription.

Keys

Modifiers are side-aware, so Right ⌥ is not Left ⌥. The chord builds as keys go down and commits when you let go, which is how ⌃ + Right ⌥ stays recordable.

What the tab strip could not do

  • A source list scales; three tabs do not. Once diagnostics come out of the menu bar there are more than three areas worth exposing, and the sidebar gives each one a name instead of burying it. It also gives History a badge, so the count is visible without opening it.
  • The sidebar is the icon's ground at full height, with the lockup as its masthead — same size, same gradients and same position as in Setup and History. The mark's colours are the icon's own stops rather than approximations, so the logo in the window and the tile in the Dock are one object.
  • Rows live in grouped boxes. Label outside, hairline-separated rows inside — the System Settings shape. Explanatory copy sits inside the box it belongs to instead of floating beside a field.
  • Reception is one group, not two scattered indicators. "Are the keys arriving" and "is audio arriving" answer the same question — why is nothing happening — so they sit together, above the count of presses another app stole.
  • Blue is spent twice: the selected sidebar row, and the receipt pill while the chord is held. The meter's hot bars are the same peak, doing the job they do in the icon.
